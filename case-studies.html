CASE 01 · Payments

Recurring billing bridge

The situation

A subscription business had billing state in Stripe and Chargify, and deal stage and revenue reporting in HubSpot. Sales had no live view of billing health, so renewal conversations happened blind.

What we built

A Laravel backend receiving Stripe and Chargify webhooks, verifying every signature, de-duplicating on event ID, and queuing updates into HubSpot deals and custom objects with automatic retries on failure.

The result

Sales sees live subscription status – invoices, upgrades, churn risk – directly on the HubSpot deal record, without switching tools or asking finance for a spreadsheet.

StripeChargifyLaravelWebhooksCustom Objects
CASE 02 · Identity

Identity-aware CRM

The situation

Auth0 handled login and roles, but HubSpot contact records had no idea who was actually signed in, what plan they were on, or what tier of support they qualified for.

What we built

An event-driven sync that listens for Auth0 lifecycle events (sign-up, upgrade, downgrade, cancellation) and updates HubSpot contact lifecycle stage and custom properties in real time, with signature-verified webhooks on both ends.

The result

Support reps see the right account context the moment a ticket comes in, and marketing can segment by actual plan and role instead of stale, manually-updated fields.

Auth0SSOLifecycle StagesWebhook Security
CASE 03 · Booking

Booking-to-revenue pipeline

The situation

A wellness business ran bookings through Mindbody, but that data was invisible inside HubSpot – sales and support had no visibility into who’d booked, attended, or missed a session.

What we built

An n8n workflow polling and receiving Mindbody events, writing bookings as HubSpot timeline events, and triggering workflows for no-shows, renewal reminders and upsell windows based on session history.

The result

Follow-ups happen automatically at the right moment instead of relying on someone remembering to check a separate booking system.

Mindbodyn8nTimeline EventsAutomation
CASE 04 · Real Estate

Commercial property deal pipeline

The situation

A commercial real estate team needed deals to track at the building, unit and lease level – something HubSpot’s default deal object couldn’t represent on its own.

What we built

Custom HubSpot objects modeling properties, units and leases, linked to deals and associated with contacts, so a single deal could reflect a multi-unit negotiation accurately.

The result

Deal reporting finally matched how the team actually thought about their pipeline – by building and unit, not just by a flat dollar amount.

Custom ObjectsReal EstateDeal Pipeline
CASE 05 · Migration

Legacy CRM migration

The situation

A business needed to move years of contact and deal history off a legacy CRM and into HubSpot, without losing data integrity or breaking active workflows mid-migration.

What we built

A field-mapped, validated migration script that ran in batches, respected HubSpot’s rate limits, logged every record for auditability, and let the team verify a sample before committing the full run.

The result

A clean cutover with a full audit trail, and no history lost in the move – a common risk in CRM migrations done by hand.

MigrationData IntegrityRate Limits
CASE 06 · Reporting

Bi-directional SQL sync for trustworthy reporting

The situation

The CRM and the backing SQL database drifted out of sync. Records updated on one side didn’t reliably reach the other, so nobody trusted reports built on HubSpot data and the team reconciled by hand every week.

What we built

Custom middleware syncing both directions on a time-windowed schedule, with per-field conflict rules deciding the source of truth, plus retries and logging so nothing failed silently at volume.

The result

Both systems stay aligned with zero dropped records, and reporting is trustworthy again – no more manual reconciliation before a board meeting.

SQLPHP/LaravelAWSWebhooks

Operational and CRM data stay in step automatically, removing manual re-entry and the errors that came with it.

CASE 08 · Finance

Stripe to HubSpot financial automation

The situation

Billing lived entirely in Stripe while the revenue picture lived in HubSpot, so deal records never reflected real payment and lifecycle events. Finance and sales reconciled by hand.

What we built

A pipeline mapping Stripe billing and lifecycle events – new customers, successful and failed payments, subscription changes, Apple Pay transactions – straight into HubSpot deals, handling multi-tiered and international transactions and idempotent so retries never double-count.

Deal and revenue data reflect reality without manual reconciliation between finance and sales.

CASE 12 · Platform

Custom middleware for high-volume syncs

The situation

Native connectors couldn’t handle the volume or logic needed. Syncs hit HubSpot API rate limits and dropped data during spikes, and the required business logic wasn’t available out of the box.

What we built

Resilient middleware that respects HubSpot rate limits with proper queuing and backoff, guarantees delivery with retries and idempotency, and encodes the custom business rules native tools couldn’t – fully logged and monitored so failures surface instead of hiding.

The result

High-volume syncs run reliably with no dropped data, even during traffic spikes.

CASE 13 · Legal / Real Estate

Time-boxed case assignment for a law firm

The situation

A real estate law firm sourced leads from Zillow, CREXi and LoopNet, but had no fast way to get each case in front of an available lawyer. Leads sat in a shared inbox, assignment was manual, and time-sensitive cases were lost to slow response.

What we built

Zapier captures and parses incoming leads from Zillow, CREXi and LoopNet straight into HubSpot. A custom-coded HubSpot workflow assigns each case to a lawyer and opens a fixed acceptance window – the lawyer can accept or decline directly; if nobody responds in time, the workflow automatically reassigns the case to the next lawyer in rotation. On acceptance, PandaDoc fires automatically to send the engagement agreement for signature.

The result

Cases move from lead to an engaged lawyer in minutes instead of sitting in an inbox, nothing is lost to an unresponsive assignee, and clients get their engagement paperwork the moment a lawyer accepts.

ZapierHubSpot Custom WorkflowsPandaDocZillowCREXiLoopNet
CASE 14 · Enterprise

Enterprise CRM & ERP sync

The situation

Businesses already running Salesforce, Dynamics 365, Business Central or NetSuite needed HubSpot in the mix – for marketing, for a lighter-weight sales motion, or during a phased move off the legacy system – without losing what already worked in the system of record.

What we built

Field-mapped sync layers between each ERP/CRM and HubSpot, respecting the source system’s object model instead of forcing a generic mapping, with full audit logging so finance and ops could trust every record that crossed over.

The result

HubSpot became a real front end on top of the enterprise system of record, not a disconnected second copy of the data everyone had to remember to update.
