Five ways this plays out in practice

CASE 01 · Payments

Recurring billing bridge

The situation

A subscription business had billing state in Stripe and Chargify, and deal stage and revenue reporting in HubSpot. Sales had no live view of billing health, so renewal conversations happened blind.

What we built

A Laravel backend receiving Stripe and Chargify webhooks, verifying every signature, de-duplicating on event ID, and queuing updates into HubSpot deals and custom objects with automatic retries on failure.

The result

Sales sees live subscription status – invoices, upgrades, churn risk – directly on the HubSpot deal record, without switching tools or asking finance for a spreadsheet.

CASE 02 · Identity

Identity-aware CRM

The situation

Auth0 handled login and roles, but HubSpot contact records had no idea who was actually signed in, what plan they were on, or what tier of support they qualified for.

What we built

An event-driven sync that listens for Auth0 lifecycle events (sign-up, upgrade, downgrade, cancellation) and updates HubSpot contact lifecycle stage and custom properties in real time, with signature-verified webhooks on both ends.

The result

Support reps see the right account context the moment a ticket comes in, and marketing can segment by actual plan and role instead of stale, manually-updated fields.

CASE 03 · Booking

Booking-to-revenue pipeline

The situation

A wellness business ran bookings through Mindbody, but that data was invisible inside HubSpot – sales and support had no visibility into who’d booked, attended, or missed a session.

What we built

An n8n workflow polling and receiving Mindbody events, writing bookings as HubSpot timeline events, and triggering workflows for no-shows, renewal reminders and upsell windows based on session history.

The result

Follow-ups happen automatically at the right moment instead of relying on someone remembering to check a separate booking system.

CASE 04 · Real Estate

Commercial property deal pipeline

The situation

A commercial real estate team needed deals to track at the building, unit and lease level – something HubSpot’s default deal object couldn’t represent on its own.

What we built

Custom HubSpot objects modeling properties, units and leases, linked to deals and associated with contacts, so a single deal could reflect a multi-unit negotiation accurately.

The result

Deal reporting finally matched how the team actually thought about their pipeline – by building and unit, not just by a flat dollar amount.

CASE 05 · Migration

Legacy CRM migration

The situation

A business needed to move years of contact and deal history off a legacy CRM and into HubSpot, without losing data integrity or breaking active workflows mid-migration.

What we built

A field-mapped, validated migration script that ran in batches, respected HubSpot’s rate limits, logged every record for auditability, and let the team verify a sample before committing the full run.

The result

A clean cutover with a full audit trail, and no history lost in the move – a common risk in CRM migrations done by hand.
